jxl-oxide and the Rust crates compiled into its WebAssembly build
=================================================================

Airgap decodes JPEG XL with the jxl-oxide-wasm package (jxl-oxide 0.12.6),
a JPEG XL decoder written in Rust from the specification by Wonwoo Choi and
the jxl-oxide contributors. Its WebAssembly binary also contains these
crates, each with the licence shown:

  jxl-oxide workspace (jxl-oxide, jxl-render, jxl-frame, jxl-modular,
    jxl-vardct, jxl-color, jxl-bitstream, jxl-coding, jxl-grid, jxl-jbr,
    jxl-image, jxl-oxide-wasm)          MIT or Apache-2.0
  png 0.18.1 (image-rs contributors)    MIT or Apache-2.0
  fdeflate 0.3.7 (The image-rs Developers) MIT or Apache-2.0
  flate2 1.1.9 (Alex Crichton)          MIT or Apache-2.0
  miniz_oxide 0.8.9 (Frommi, oyvindln)  MIT or Zlib or Apache-2.0
  once_cell 1.21.4 (Aleksey Kladov)     MIT or Apache-2.0
  tracing-core 0.1.36 (Tokio Contributors) MIT
  brotli-decompressor 5.0.0 (Dropbox)   BSD-3-Clause or MIT
  wasm-bindgen 0.2.122                  MIT or Apache-2.0

Where a crate offers a choice, Airgap redistributes it under the MIT licence,
reproduced below with every copyright line the crates carry. The Apache
alternative is in licenses/Apache-2.0.txt. brotli-decompressor is reproduced
under its BSD-3-Clause licence, after the MIT text.
