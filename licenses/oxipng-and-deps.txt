oxipng and the code compiled into its WebAssembly build
========================================================

Airgap's optional "optimise PNG further" step uses oxipng 9.1.1 (by Joshua
Holmer and contributors), compiled single-threaded to WebAssembly by the
@jsquash/oxipng package. The binary also contains:

  libdeflate (C, by Eric Biggers)            MIT
  libdeflater 1.20.0, libdeflate-sys 1.20.0  Apache-2.0 (Adam Kewley)
  bitvec 1.0.1, funty 2.0.0, wyz 0.5.1       MIT (myrrlyn / Alexander Payne)
  radium 0.7.0 (Nika Layzell)                MIT
  tap 1.0.1 (Elliot Linder)                  MIT
  rgb 0.8.37 (Kornel Lesinski)               MIT
  indexmap 2.2.6, equivalent 1.0.1           MIT or Apache-2.0
  hashbrown 0.14.5 (Amanieu d'Antras)        MIT or Apache-2.0
  log 0.4.21, rustc-hash 1.1.0               MIT or Apache-2.0 (The Rust Project Developers)
  wasm-bindgen                               MIT or Apache-2.0

The Apache-2.0 components (and the Apache alternative of the dual-licensed
ones) are covered by licenses/Apache-2.0.txt. The MIT text follows, with the
copyright lines of every MIT component, then libdeflate's licence verbatim.
